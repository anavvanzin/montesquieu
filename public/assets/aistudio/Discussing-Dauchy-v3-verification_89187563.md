## Private notes on Dauchy’s manuscript (not for the slides)

To read before 11 November. None of these points affects your argument.

1. **Place of death (¶1).** He writes that Montesquieu was born at La Brède « et y décèdera ». Montesquieu died **in Paris**, at his rue Saint-Dominique home, and was buried at Saint-Sulpice.
2. **Considérations sur les richesses de l’Espagne (¶9).** He says Montesquieu « avait d’ailleurs déjà publié » this study. As far as I can establish it stayed unpublished in his lifetime and first appeared in 1910, edited by Paul Bonnefon. He reused material from it in *De l’esprit des lois*.
3. **Watson at Edinburgh (¶11).** This one is correct. Watson held the Edinburgh chair of civil law from 1968; my initial doubt was wrong.
4. **German translation 1782 (¶3).** Possibly too late, since an earlier German translation may exist. **Unresolved**: I found a title (*Des Herrn von Montesquiou Werk Von den Gesetzen*) but no reliable date.
5. **Weber and Teubner quotations (¶12, ¶14).** They have no reference in the manuscript. Teubner’s source is identified above. The Weber passage is unidentified.
